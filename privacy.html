Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

We collect as little as we can. This page explains what, and why.

What we collect

  • Account details (only if you sign up): your email, a display name, and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password.
  • Saved designs and reviews you choose to save or post.
  • Payment information: handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. We keep a record that a payment was completed.
  • Basic technical data such as your IP address, used briefly to prevent abuse (for example, limiting repeated requests).

What stays on your device

Your color theme, your recent work, and a short-lived record of a paid download are stored in your own browser. Designs you make without an account are not sent to us.

How we use it

To run the site, save your designs, process payments, answer your emails, and keep Invitely secure. We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

Who else sees it

  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Vercel hosts the site and stores account data.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts, so your browser contacts Google when pages load.

Your choices

You can delete your account and saved designs from the account menu at any time. You can also email zliblich@gmail.com and ask us to delete or send you your data.

Children

Invitely is meant for adults planning events. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions? zliblich@gmail.com.
