Refund policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Invitely sells digital downloads: a finished invitation picture or video. You pay once for each download. There are no subscriptions and nothing renews.

Our promise: if something goes wrong, we will make it right. Email zliblich@gmail.com within 14 days of your purchase.

We will refund you if

  • You were charged but did not receive your download.
  • You were charged more than once for the same download.
  • The file is broken or does not match the design you paid for, and we cannot fix it quickly.

We usually cannot refund if

  • You successfully downloaded the file and simply changed your mind. Because a download is delivered instantly and cannot be returned, these sales are final.
  • The names, dates or spelling you typed contain a mistake. A paid download stays open for 48 hours so you can fix it and download again at no extra charge.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original card through Stripe. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

Free designs

Free designs and founding-member downloads cost nothing, so there is nothing to refund.
